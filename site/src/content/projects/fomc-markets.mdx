<Section id="method" title="Method">
  <div class="prose">
    <p><strong>Question.</strong> How far ahead, and how accurately, do prediction markets price Federal Reserve decisions, and do the two venues disagree?</p>
    <p><strong>Data.</strong> Every "Fed decision in &lt;month&gt;" event on Polymarket and every <code>KXFEDDECISION</code> event on Kalshi ({num(facts.decision_markets)} markets), plus the rate-level, hike-count and dot-plot markets on both. Daily history is backfilled from Polymarket's price-history endpoint and Kalshi's candlesticks; from September 16, 2026 the snapshot pipeline adds two observations a day including order books.</p>
    <p><strong>Definitions.</strong> Both platforms list the same five outcomes per meeting; they are mapped to one grid (cut 50+, cut 25, hold, hike 25, hike 50+) and rolled up to cut / hold / hike for charts. Probability = order-book mid where quoted, else the displayed price. Expected move = Σ probability × bps. Decision dates follow the Fed's published calendar; 2027 dates are provisional.</p>
  </div>
</Section>

<Section id="evaluation" title="Evaluation (pre-registered)">
  <div class="prose">
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Per meeting:</strong> multi-outcome Brier score of each platform's final pre-decision distribution (last observation before 17:30 UTC on decision day), and the probability assigned to the realised outcome.</li>
      <li><strong>Lead time:</strong> the number of days before the decision when the realised outcome first became and stayed the modal outcome on each platform.</li>
      <li><strong>Baselines:</strong> "no change" every meeting, and "same as last meeting", scored the same way.</li>
      <li><strong>Cross-platform:</strong> the daily gap in P(hike) and whether it closes into the meeting.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Market reaction (Treasury yields and equity moves on decision day versus the surprise) is planned once a market-data key is configured; it is not part of this page yet.</p>
  </div>
</Section>
