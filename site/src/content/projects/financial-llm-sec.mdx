<Section id="method" title="Method">
  <div class="prose">
    <p><strong>Data.</strong> {num(facts.dataset.filings)} filings ({facts.dataset.forms.join(', ')}) for {facts.dataset.tickers} S&amp;P 500 companies were pulled from EDGAR, summarised by {facts.dataset.teacher}, and turned into {num(facts.dataset.qa_pairs)} analyst-style question–answer pairs. The teacher's answers are the training targets and the evaluation references, so the benchmark measures agreement with the teacher, not ground truth.</p>
    <p><strong>Training.</strong> LoRA adapters (rank 8) trained with HF PEFT on {facts.training.hardware}, sharded with PyTorch/XLA SPMD FSDPv2 in bf16. Phase 1 used Gemma 2 27B; Phase 2 re-ran the identical recipe (same data, rank, alpha, mesh, hyperparameters) on Gemma 4 31B, changing only the model id, the chat template and the KV-cache setup.</p>
    <p><strong>Evaluation.</strong> {facts.test_items} held-out items scored with BERTScore (precision, recall, F1) against the reference answers, for four models: each base and each base plus the adapter. Differences are tested with a paired t-test on per-item F1.</p>
  </div>
</Section>

      <li><strong>Base upgrade is additive:</strong> Gemma 4 + LoRA beats Gemma 2 + LoRA by {facts.deltas_pct.gemma2_v2_to_gemma4_v2}%.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Limitations: {facts.test_items} items is a small test set and the confidence intervals are wide; BERTScore rewards lexical and semantic overlap with a teacher-written reference, not factual correctness against the filing; the QA pairs and the references come from the same teacher, so the adapter partly learns the teacher's style. A retrieval-grounded factuality check against the source filings is the natural next evaluation.</p>
