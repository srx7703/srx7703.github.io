<Section id="method" title="Method">
  <div class="prose">
    <p><strong>Question.</strong> What is the listed solid-state battery world being priced at, and how much of that price rests on a product that is not being sold in volume yet?</p>
    <p><strong>Pool.</strong> {facts.n_listings} listings across {facts.n_issuers} companies in mainland China, the United States, Japan, Korea, Hong Kong and the United Kingdom, in three groups: the pure plays, the cell makers with a solid-state roadmap, and the materials and equipment suppliers. Private developers are named in the shipments and capacity tables but cannot be valued, so they are not in the pool.</p>
    <p><strong>Prices and consensus.</strong> As on the optical page: Yahoo Finance for prices and non-Chinese consensus, East Money's per-broker forecasts for the A-shares, SEC XBRL and East Money quarterly reports and Yahoo for trailing earnings, FRED for exchange rates. Fiscal years are restated onto calendar years by month overlap, which matters here for the Japanese names, whose years end in March.</p>
    <p><strong>Definitions.</strong> Trailing PE is market capitalisation over trailing-twelve-month attributable profit. Forward PE is price over calendar-year consensus EPS, with the consensus converted into the price currency first. Growth is the consensus for {y1} over the consensus for {y0}. Price to sales is market value over trailing revenue, shown only for the pure plays and only where there is revenue.</p>
    <p><strong>What is deliberately missing.</strong> No solid-state market share, because a market share of a market that does not exist is not a number. No negative PE: a company forecast to lose money gets a written reason instead. No estimate of how much of any company's value is "the solid-state option", which would be an opinion rather than a measurement.</p>
  </div>
</Section>

<Section id="evaluation" title="Evaluation (pre-registered)">
  <div class="prose">
    <p>The scoring rules were committed in <a href={plan.url}>docs/EVALUATION_PLAN.md</a> (git blob <code>{plan.short}</code>, <a href={plan.history}>history</a>) before the first snapshot was published.</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Consensus accuracy.</strong> The calendar-{y0} consensus against reported {y0} results, split by source, track and coverage depth, with two naive baselines. Scored from spring {y0 + 1}.</li>
      <li><strong>Revision persistence.</strong> Whether a consensus that moved up keeps moving up, as a transition matrix, using only true revision series.</li>
      <li><strong>Valuation dispersion.</strong> The quartile spread of forward PE each quarter, next to the count of priced listings.</li>
      <li><strong>Same earnings, two prices.</strong> The A-line over H-line multiple for the dual-listed issuers, quarterly.</li>
      <li><strong>Computed share against cited share.</strong> Rank correlation and median gap between the two bases, with the gap attributed.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
</Section>
