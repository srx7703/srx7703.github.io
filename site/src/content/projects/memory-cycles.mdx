<Section id="method" title="Method">

**Price calendar.** The raw ECOS responses from the registration pull are committed. The pipeline parses them
into a snapshot and runs the registered rule ten ways: contract currency at three thresholds, plus the dollar
and won bases at the main threshold, for both items. Every turn and every phase is checked against the research
outputs before anything is written.

**Stock side.** Month-end closes, adjusted for splits and capital reductions, come from exchange and vendor
sources whose terms forbid redistribution. They were used on the owner's machine to compute each company's
peak, trough, lead and multiples in each cycle, and only those derived months, leads, ratios and flags were
committed. The page is rebuilt from that frozen table. A guard refuses to publish if the recomputed price
calendar ever disagrees with the peaks the frozen table was measured against.

**Checks on the closes** (done locally, before registration). Every extreme was compared with a second source,
with official anchors from Micron's 10-K ranges, the Taiwan Stock Exchange and KRX. Korean closes in the month
after KRX opened its after-market session are the official regular-session closes. Elpida's full series was
rebuilt from daily tables and cross-checked.

<Section id="limits" title="Limits">

<ul>
  <li><strong>Contract prices lag spot prices.</strong> Spot DRAM prices have peaked months before this index in recent cycles. A lead measured against spot prices would be longer.</li>
  <li><strong>Turns are uncertain by a quarter in recent years.</strong> Contract prices now reset mostly once a quarter, so a turn dated to one month may belong to the months around it.</li>
  <li><strong>Levels, not detrended values.</strong> Dating a series that falls steeply every year on its levels makes upswings shorter. A detrended calendar was not registered and is not shown.</li>
  <li><strong>{d.up_legs_at_min_phase.length + n.up_legs_at_min_phase.length} rising phases sit exactly at the minimum length.</strong> The DRAM rise to {ym(d.up_legs_at_min_phase[0])} and the flash rise to {ym(n.up_legs_at_min_phase[0])} last exactly {facts.rule.min_phase} months. One month shorter and the rule would delete them.</li>
  <li><strong>A stock high can be booked to the wrong cycle.</strong> The stock-peak window runs to the next price trough, so a high made early in the next rally counts against the previous peak.</li>
  <li><strong>The stock side cannot be re-verified in CI.</strong> The closes stay local. What is public is their hashes, the derived table and the tests that bind them.</li>
</ul>

</Section>
