<Section id="method" title="Method">
  <div class="prose">
    <p><strong>Question.</strong> How do the two largest prediction-market venues price the 2026 midterms, how far apart are they, and how well will they have done once results are in?</p>
    <p><strong>Data.</strong> A scheduled GitHub Actions job snapshots both platforms twice a day. Polymarket: every market tagged <em>Midterms</em> on the Gamma API ({num(facts.markets_tracked.polymarket)} markets in the latest full run), plus CLOB order books for the headline events and every state Senate and Governor winner market. Kalshi: every open <em>Elections</em> event with markets closing between November 2026 and November 2027 ({num(facts.markets_tracked.kalshi)} markets), plus order books for the control, seat-count and state-race series. No API keys are involved; both APIs are public.</p>
    <p><strong>Definitions.</strong> The probability shown is the order-book mid when both sides are quoted, otherwise the platform's displayed YES price. Gap = Polymarket minus Kalshi, in percentage points. Order-book depth is summed within 5 and 10 cents of the touch on each side. Raw responses are archived unchanged; all tables are rebuilt from them.</p>
    <p><strong>Pipeline.</strong> <code>pipelines/predmarkets/snapshot.py</code> (ingest, pandera-validated) → parquet snapshots + dimension deltas → <code>publish.py</code> (Polars marts and the facts file this page is templated from). "Actively quoted" means both sides quoted, spread of at most 10 cents and some volume; the raw universe includes many dormant markets. Every number on this page is read from <code>data/facts/midterms.json</code>; none is typed by hand.</p>
  </div>
</Section>

<Section id="evaluation" title="Evaluation (pre-registered)">
  <div class="prose">
    <p>Results are scored after election day against the plan below, committed before it in <a href={plan.url}>docs/EVALUATION_PLAN.md</a> (git blob <code>{plan.short}</code>, <a href={plan.history}>history</a>) so the scoring rules cannot be tuned to the outcome.</p>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Accuracy:</strong> Brier score and log loss of each platform's final pre-election price for every resolved market, overall and by market type (control, seat count, state race).</li>
      <li><strong>Calibration:</strong> reliability diagram in 10 probability bins, plus sharpness.</li>
      <li><strong>Lead time:</strong> the number of days before election day when a market last crossed to the side that eventually won and stayed there.</li>
      <li><strong>Cross-platform:</strong> the distribution of the Polymarket–Kalshi gap over time and whether it converges in the final week.</li>
      <li><strong>Baseline:</strong> Cook Political Report ratings mapped to fixed probabilities (Solid 0.95, Likely 0.85, Lean 0.70, Toss-up 0.50), scored the same way.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Known limitations: order books are only captured for markets priced between 2% and 98%; snapshots are twice daily, so intraday moves are not observed; Polymarket volume is on-chain USDC notional while Kalshi volume is in contracts.</p>
  </div>
</Section>
