<Section id="margins" title="The squeeze hit the brands and most mainland makers; the purest Taiwan makers barely moved" wide>

Selling a full rack means reselling a great deal of NVIDIA silicon at little mark-up, so gross margin falls as AI's
share of sales rises. What the filings add is where it falls. The Taiwan makers were already building to cost, mostly at single-digit
gross margins, and the purest of them (Wiwynn, Inventec, Hon Hai) have barely moved. Most brands' and mainland makers'
margins have fallen toward them, while the contract manufacturers' rose: they sell assembly and testing, not the GPUs.

<Section id="method" title="Method">

**Taiwan.** Monthly revenue pages for every month since 2016 and the quarterly income-statement summaries since 2019
are read from MOPS and archived as served. Quarterly figures are year-to-date and are differenced within each year.
**United States.** Each 10-Q and 10-K since 2019 is read from its XBRL instance, keeping dimensional facts, because the
server segments of Dell and HPE exist only there. Dell's servers and networking line is its Infrastructure Solutions
Group less storage. **Mainland.** East Money's copy of the statutory statements. **Lenovo** files in Hong Kong; its
quarters are read from its results announcements by hand. **Valuation** comes from this site's valuation pipeline
(Yahoo Finance and East Money consensus), frozen on the day the rules were registered.
**Curated layer.** Customers, capabilities, private companies, IDC and TrendForce figures were collected by research
passes and then re-checked against the cited pages by separate passes that tried to refute them; corrections are
recorded in the files.

<Section id="limits" title="Limits">

<ul>
  <li><strong>Company-level margins mix businesses.</strong> Lenovo is mostly PCs, Hon Hai mostly consumer electronics; their margins say little about servers, which is why Q2 grades only the server-heavy makers.</li>
  <li><strong>Fiscal calendars differ.</strong> Dell and HPE quarters end a month after the calendar quarter they are placed in.</li>
  <li><strong>Capabilities are hard to verify.</strong> Many are claims in a company's own release, marked as such, and absence of a source is not evidence of absence.</li>
  <li><strong>Shares from IDC are of all servers</strong>, not AI servers alone, and IDC does not split the direct-from-maker line by maker.</li>
</ul>
