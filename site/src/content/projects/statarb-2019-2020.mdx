<Section id="method" title="Method">
  <div class="prose">
    <p><strong>Universe and data.</strong> Top-{facts.universe.candidates} companies by market capitalisation (snapshot 2026-05-04), daily adjusted closes from Yahoo Finance, December 2018 to December 2020. {facts.universe.dropped} names are dropped for missing history or non-NYSE trading calendars, leaving {facts.universe.tickers} tickers over {facts.universe.days} NYSE trading days. Log returns are winsorised at 1% and 99% per ticker. Survivorship bias from using today's ranking is acknowledged, not corrected.</p>
    <p><strong>Models.</strong> For each target stock, the return is regressed on the other {facts.universe.tickers - 1} (OLS), on the first {facts.pca.k} principal components explaining {pct(facts.pca.cum_var, 1)} of variance (PCA; PC1 correlates {facts.pca.corr_pc1_market.toFixed(3)} with an equal-weight market proxy), or on the other stocks with an L1 penalty (LASSO, on average {facts.lasso_hedges_mean} non-zero hedges per target). Fits use 2019 only.</p>
    <p><strong>Signal and backtest.</strong> Residuals are z-scored over a 60-day rolling window; enter at |z| = 2, exit at 0.5. Positions are dollar-neutral: $1 in the target against $1 in the hedge basket. Ornstein–Uhlenbeck half-lives of the residuals average {Math.min(...facts.half_life.map((h) => h.mean_days)).toFixed(0)}–{Math.max(...facts.half_life.map((h) => h.mean_days)).toFixed(0)} days across the three models. Benchmarks: Engle–Granger pairs trading (five lowest p-value pairs from the training year) and equal-weight buy-and-hold.</p>
  </div>
</Section>

<Section id="evaluation" title="Evaluation">
  <div class="prose">
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Overfitting</strong> ({facts.basis.train_test}): train Sharpe {Math.min(...facts.train_test.map((r) => r.train)).toFixed(1)}–{Math.max(...facts.train_test.map((r) => r.train)).toFixed(1)} versus test {Math.min(...facts.train_test.map((r) => r.test)).toFixed(1)} to +{Math.max(...facts.train_test.map((r) => r.test)).toFixed(1)}; {facts.smallest_gap} has the smallest gap, as the L1 penalty predicts.</li>
      <li><strong>Significance:</strong> bootstrap 95% intervals for every Sharpe include zero; Memmel tests of LASSO against OLS, PCA and pairs do not reject equal Sharpe (p from {Math.min(...facts.memmel.map((m) => m.p)).toFixed(2)} to {Math.max(...facts.memmel.map((m) => m.p)).toFixed(2)}).</li>
      <li><strong>Look-ahead audit:</strong> {facts.lookahead_audit.passed} of {facts.lookahead_audit.checks} checks pass, including a deliberately leaky variant that should and does blow up the Sharpe, and a perfect-foresight oracle as an upper bound.</li>
      <li><strong>Robustness</strong> ({facts.basis.rolling}): a rolling refit lifts OLS from {facts.rolling[0].static} to {facts.rolling[0].rolling} and LASSO from {facts.rolling[1].static} to {facts.rolling[1].rolling} , which locates the loss in stale betas rather than in the signal. These residual-direct figures are not comparable with the portfolio-level Sharpe ratios above.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Limitations: one test year, one crisis, five-pair benchmark, no borrow costs or market impact, and a universe chosen with hindsight.</p>
  </div>
