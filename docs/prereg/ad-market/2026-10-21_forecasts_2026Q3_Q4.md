Source data: quarterly advertising revenue from each company's SEC 10-Q/10-K XBRL, 2021Q1 to 2026Q2 (`ad_revenue_quarterly_usd_bn.csv`, SHA-256 `b01adbcbef8f214bb353df979039628fbaed5652bbf380846ddd9beab87a3450`). ## Rule A: point forecasts with 80% intervals (mechanical)

Forecast for a quarter = the same quarter one year earlier x (1 + year-over-year growth of 2026Q2). The 80% interval is this rule's own back-test error, 10th to 90th percentile of log errors over 2023Q1 onwards, pooled across companies: one quarter ahead -5.3% to +6.3% (n=191), two quarters ahead -6.3% to +11.2% (n=176).

Series definitions: Google = Google advertising (Search & other + YouTube ads + Google Network); Meta = advertising; Amazon = advertising services; Microsoft = search and news advertising; Snap, Pinterest, Trade Desk, Magnite, PubMatic, Unity = total revenue; AppLovin = advertising segment (total revenue after the 2025 sale of its apps business); Reddit = advertising.

**Scoring A.** Over the 30 intervals, the expected hit rate is about 80%. A hit rate of 60% or lower means the rule is miscalibrated in this period; 95% or higher means the intervals were too wide. Each company is scored on the first figure it reports for the quarter.

## Rule B: directional calls for 2026Q3 (judgement, each true or false)

B1. Google's share of the video's nine-company subset in 2026Q3 is below its 2025Q3 share (49.2%).
B2. Meta's share of the same subset in 2026Q3 is above its 2025Q3 share (33.2%).
B3. Trade Desk's 2026Q3 revenue grows less than 10% year over year.
B4. AppLovin's 2026Q3 advertising revenue is larger than Snap's 2026Q3 total revenue.
B5. Reddit's 2026Q3 advertising revenue grows more than 40% year over year.
B6. The open-web group (Google Network + Trade Desk + Magnite + PubMatic) grows more slowly year over year in 2026Q3 than the walled-garden group (Google Search + YouTube + Meta + Amazon + Microsoft + Snap + Pinterest + Reddit).
B7. Google Network's 2026Q3 revenue is lower than its 2025Q3 revenue (a fourth year of flat-to-down open-web revenue for Google).

The nine-company subset is the video's: Google, Meta, Amazon, Microsoft, Snap, AppLovin, Pinterest, Trade Desk, Reddit, with the series definitions above.
